Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Ozone UPVC Doors & Windows (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and protects the personal information you share with us through our website (ozonekerala.com), phone calls, and WhatsApp messages.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, submit an enquiry, or contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and site location or address.
  • Project information: plans, measurements, design preferences, and property details you share for an estimate on UPVC doors and windows.
  • Technical data: only if we add website analytics, basic browser and device information, pages visited, and referral sources, used to improve the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiries, calls, and WhatsApp messages.
  • Arrange on-site measurements and consultations.
  • Prepare quotations and carry out fabrication and installation orders.
  • Improve our products, website, and customer support.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with our own fabrication, measurement, and installation teams, or with trusted logistics partners where needed to complete your order, or where Indian law requires it.

4. Third-Party Links

The website links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Google. When you use those platforms, their privacy policies also apply.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your contact and project details from unauthorised access. We keep project records only as long as needed for order completion, warranty support, and legal compliance.

6. Your Rights and Contact

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete your personal details at any time. Contact us at +91 8089113701 or through ozonekerala.com, or visit our office in Karunagappally, Kollam, Kerala.
